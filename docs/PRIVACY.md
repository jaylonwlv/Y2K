# Y2K Home Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Y2K Home is a home-screen theme app: wallpapers, widgets and icons. It's built to work without
collecting anything about you.

## What we collect

Nothing. Y2K Home has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We don't run servers that
receive your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Calendar.** If you connect your calendar, the Calendar widget reads today's events on your
  iPhone to show them. They're never sent anywhere.
- **Photos.** Photos you pick for the Mixtape and Vibe widgets are copied into the app's own storage
  on your iPhone so the widgets can show them. Wallpapers, icons and posts you save are written to
  your photo library, and only when you ask.
- **Settings.** Your theme, widget choices and captions are stored on your iPhone.

## Weather

The Weather widget asks Apple's WeatherKit for the forecast of the city you pick. The request
contains that city's location, not yours. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy
policy. Weather data sources: https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html

## Icon profiles

"Install all at once" creates a configuration profile on your iPhone that only adds home-screen
icons. It's built on your iPhone and handed straight to Settings; it isn't uploaded anywhere, and it
can't read your data. Remove it any time in Settings › General › VPN & Device Management.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

## Children

Y2K Home doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
