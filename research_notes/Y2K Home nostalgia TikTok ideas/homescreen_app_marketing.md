# Home-Screen Customization / Widget / Theme Apps on TikTok: Case Studies, Formats and Indie Growth Playbooks

> Method note: In this session, direct page fetches (techcrunch.com, sensortower.com, david-smith.org) were blocked by network policy. Findings below come from web-search result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. Numbers should be spot-checked against the source pages before publication. Labels: **[3P estimate]** = app-intelligence estimate (Appfigures/Sensor Tower/AppGoblin); **[self-reported]** = founder/developer claim; **[vendor/anecdotal]** = marketing-tool vendor blog or secondhand anecdote, not independently verified.

## Q1. What drove the iOS 14 aesthetic home screen wave (Sept 2020), and which TikTok formats worked?

### Takeaway
iOS 14 (Sept 16, 2020) shipped third-party widgets plus a Shortcuts-based custom icon workaround. A single TikTok walkthrough tutorial ("how to make your home screen aesthetic" using Widgetsmith) then went viral and turned into a copycat genre. Widgetsmith went to #1 for about two weeks, and the top three customization apps took roughly 7–12M downloads in their first week. The format that worked was the **step-by-step tutorial ending on a reveal**. Twitter "how to" threads and Pinterest/TikTok setup shares fed it. Paid ads were not the driver.

### Cited Findings
- **Widgetsmith rank:** developer David Smith says Widgetsmith "quickly became the #1 App in the App Store, and remarkably stayed there for a couple of weeks." Someone told him on Twitter they had seen it on TikTok. **[self-reported]** — [Widgetsmith Five Years Later, david-smith.org (2025)](https://www.david-smith.org/blog/2025/09/18/widgetsmith-at-five/)
- **Widgetsmith lifetime downloads:** about 131M by Sept 2025, per Smith's five-year retrospective. **[self-reported]**, not independently audited — [david-smith.org](https://www.david-smith.org/blog/2025/09/18/widgetsmith-at-five/)
- Smith said the surge exceeded his total lifetime App Store downloads across 13 years of apps "in a few hours." The playbook summary frames his strategy as making the app first, shareable and beautiful enough that someone else's TikTok did the marketing. **[self-reported, via secondary]** — [BetterLaunch Founder Playbook: David Smith/Widgetsmith](https://www.betterlaunch.co/playbooks/david-smith-widgetsmith-12)
- **Appfigures:** Widgetsmith, Color Widgets and Photo Widget climbed to the top of the App Store and together saw more than 7M downloads in under a week. **[3P estimate]** — [Appfigures This Week in Apps, Sept 25, 2020](https://appfigures.com/resources/this-week-in-apps/20200925)
- **Sensor Tower (via TechCrunch, Sept 23, 2020):** the top 20 home-screen customization apps reached 5.7M installs in the days after iOS 14's release, and the top three apps accounted for 95% of them. An updated figure put the top three at more than 12.6M installs for Sept 17–23. **[3P estimate]** — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/23/top-20-ios-homescreen-customization-apps-reach-5-7m-installs-after-ios-14-release/); [iMore](https://www.imore.com/top-20-home-screen-customization-apps-hit-57-million-downloads-4-days)
- **Sensor Tower spending:** the cohort generated more than $1M in consumer spending from Sept 17–23, 2020. Widgetsmith led with more than $840K and Color Widgets followed with more than $155K. **[3P estimate]** — [Sensor Tower blog](https://sensortower.com/blog/ios-14-home-screen-app-downloads)
- **Reach by Nov 2020:** about 1 in 7 (roughly 15%) of US iPhones were using custom home-screen/widget apps. **[3P estimate]** — [MacRumors, Nov 20, 2020](https://macrumors.com/2020/11/20/custom-home-screen-apps-1-in-7-us-iphones); [iMore](https://www.imore.com/15-us-iphone-users-using-widgets-customize-ios-14)
- **The pivotal video:** a walkthrough by creator **Katarina Mogus** showed how to use Widgetsmith to make the home screen "Aesthetic AF" and was going viral at the time of the surge. **[secondary]** — [mjtsai.com, Sept 25, 2020](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2020/09/25/widgetsmith/)
- Mogus's Widgetsmith tutorial was reportedly seen more than **25M times**, after which she sold her designs on Etsy. This is attributed to The Verge but I saw it only via a secondary quote. **[secondary, unverified]** — [9to5Mac coverage cluster](https://9to5mac.com/2020/09/20/widgetsmith-ios-14-home-screen-widgets/) / [Know Your Meme: iOS 14 Homescreen Customizations](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ios-14-homescreen-customizations)
- **Trend origin:** Know Your Meme traces the trend to a Twitter thread by @Kaylasfro, "how to do the aesthetic Home Screen (iOS 14): a thread." TikTok tutorials then spread it, and other examples included themed screens such as a 15-year-old Twilight fan's setup. — [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ios-14-homescreen-customizations)
- Early adopters posted setups on Pinterest and TikTok, and iOS 14 icon packs "popped up everywhere," many sold via Gumroad/Etsy. — [Redmondpie](https://redmondpie.com/?p=438583); [Dribbble Stories, Sept 25, 2020](https://dribbble.com/stories/2020/09/25/ios-14-custom-layouts-themes); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/i-lost-my-mind-customizing-my-iphone-home-screen-for-th-1845141776)
- The trend also spread outside the US on TikTok and Instagram, for example in Brazil. — [Olhar Digital](https://olhardigital.com.br/noticia/widgets-personalizados-do-ios-14-sao-febre-no-tiktok-e-instagram/107556)

### Inferences
- The viral unit was a **tutorial with a payoff reveal**: it showed a boring stock screen, the steps (Widgetsmith + Shortcuts icons + matching wallpaper), then the finished aesthetic screen. Viewers saved it to copy the steps, so tutorial value drove saves and shares.
- **Theming by fandom or aesthetic** (Twilight, cottagecore, pastel, anime) made each video a new piece of content without changing the app. That maps directly to Y2K/Frutiger Aero sub-themes.
- The demand came from an **OS-level novelty moment**. Theme apps that are ready on OS launch day capture outsized share. This pattern repeated in 2022 (Q2), and Apple's annual September iOS release is the recurring window.
- Creator monetization (Etsy icon packs) shows the audience was willing to pay for curated packs, which validates icon-pack IAP pricing.

### Gaps
- I could not access The Verge's original article to confirm the 25M view figure, or Mogus's exact video date and URL.
- I found no hashtag-level TikTok view totals for #aesthetichomescreen or #ios14homescreen (TikTok no longer exposes them reliably).

## Q2. Follow-on waves (iOS 16 2022, Locket 2022, iOS 18 2024, iOS 26 2025) and current case studies

### Takeaway
Each new iOS customization feature produces a smaller spike than iOS 14 did. Top Widgets/Widgetable led the iOS 16 lock-screen wave, partly through Apple Search Ads. Locket Widget (Jan 2022) is the best TikTok-native widget case: a founder-run account, an original sound, then UGC copycats and a paid micro-influencer/ambassador program. For iOS 18 tinted icons and iOS 26 Liquid Glass "Clear" icons I found press coverage of the features, but no documented app chart surge or quantified TikTok trend.

### Cited Findings
**iOS 16 (Sept 2022)**
- Top Widgets was the most-downloaded app in the US, with an estimated 750K+ new downloads on iOS 16 launch Tuesday. Color Widgets was #3. **[3P estimate]** — [Appfigures, Sept 16, 2022](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220916/1-ios-16-propells-widgets-to-the-top-of-the-app-store)
- Top Widgets hit #1 free in the US, displacing BeReal. Sensor Tower said it gained about 1.3M downloads in the two days after launch. — [TechCrunch, Sept 15, 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/09/15/top-widgets-soars-to-no-1-on-the-app-store-displacing-bereal-as-ios-16-customization-takes-off); NECN reported it at #2 behind BeReal at a different snapshot — [NECN](https://www.necn.com/news/business/money-report/how-to-add-widgets-to-your-iphone-lock-screen-using-a-new-app-that-everyones-downloading/2824536/)
- Widgetable, Top Widgets, **Themify** and Widgetsmith crossed 13M combined downloads after iOS 16 (more than 1M/day), but that was "a fraction" of the iOS 14 wave. Widgetable, the leader, was "spending lots of money on search ads." Appfigures advised against entering widget apps unless "you have time and money to burn." **[3P estimate]** — [Appfigures, Sept 23, 2022](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220923/3-millions-of-downloads-later-widgets-arent-as-hot-this-time-around)

**Locket Widget (Jan 2022): TikTok-native widget launch**
- Launched Jan 1, 2022, it became #1 free in the US App Store with more than 2M users signed up by Jan 11 (Apptopia data via TechCrunch). — [TechCrunch, Jan 11, 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/11/locket-an-app-for-sharing-photos-to-friends-homescreens-hits-the-top-of-the-app-store); [iPhone in Canada (#1 Canada & US)](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2022/01/14/locket-no-1-free-app-in-canada/)
- Founder Matt Moss posted to a company TikTok account. His video got about 100K views over a couple of days, and other users made their own videos with the app and **the original sound**. One UK user's video topped **5M views in a single day**. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/11/locket-an-app-for-sharing-photos-to-friends-homescreens-hits-the-top-of-the-app-store)
- OMR later counted 561K views on the founder clip and 6.5M+ combined across four Locket videos. It cited about 600K downloads/day and 5M+ downloads (Airnow data). **[3P estimate]** — [OMR](https://omr.com/de/locket-social-app-tiktok/)
- Appfigures estimated 1.5M App Store downloads since the surge began, a conflict with OMR/Airnow's 5M+. **[3P estimates conflict]** — [Appfigures, Jan 14, 2022](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220114/5-new-year-new-widget-same-growth-strategy)
- **Later growth model:** Locket seeded small influencers, who posted on their own profiles and generated millions of views. It then shifted to an internal growth team posting the same format several times a day. A secondhand claim credits Locket with 250M+ TikTok views and #3 in Social Networking. **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Shortimize: Locket's TikTok influencer strategy](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/locketapps-tiktok-influencer-strategy-for-gaining-250m-views); [Threadreader thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1820390686039937099.html)
- **Ambassador mechanics (Locket/Airbuds playbook):** one person runs the program and posts example videos; recruit small creators (800–10K followers); pay ambassadors weekly so you can pivot fast. Locket did not require the app name in ambassador usernames. **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Growth Kit Handbook (Notion)](https://growthkithandbook.notion.site/The-Exact-Strategy-Airbuds-and-Locket-Used-to-Scale-121d142e96d08179a8cbd008b29e17e6); related: [Shortimize: Airbuds 300 TikToks a day to #2](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/airbuds-300-tiktok-a-day-strategy-to-2-on-the-appstore)

**Current theme-app incumbents (estimates only; no TikTok-specific data found)**
- **Brass – Icon Themes & Widgets:** freemium, with about 10 Premium IAP tiers from $1.49 to $39.99. An aggregator lists 10M+ downloads and 4.6★ from 188K ratings. Its onboarding leads with the "end state" (finished aesthetic home screens). **[3P/aggregator]** — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1533158013); [ScreensDesign showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/brass-icon-themes-widgets); [MWM](https://mwm.ai/ko/apps/brass-icon-themes-widgets/1533158013)
- **ScreenKit (Twinstar Creatives, @screenkit.app):** about 3.8M installs, 205K MAU, under $10K/month revenue est., 100% IAP. **[3P estimate]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1533393982)
- **Aesthetic Kit: Photo Widget:** about 247K installs, under $10K/month est., 77% ads. A same-named theme app gates most content behind a subscription and adds an AI theme generator (it's unclear whether these are the same app). **[3P estimate]** — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/1542205620/trends); [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/aesthetic-kit-widgets-themes)
- Icons8's "Aesthetic Icons" app markets 4,500 designer icons. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1537383985)

**iOS 18 (2024) and iOS 26 (2025)**
- iOS 18 added Dark and Tinted icon modes. Auto-generated dark icons sometimes look bad, and some outlets framed the look as "ugly." Third-party packs for the look sell on Gumroad, e.g. 150+ dark icons for $15 and a 700-icon pack for £4. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2024/07/15/this-is-how-apple-is-modding-your-app-icons-in-ios-18s-dark-mode); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/iOS-18-lets-you-make-your-iPhone-home-screen-ugly-but-this-is-exactly-what-you-might-want_id162725); [Gumroad example](https://soltwagner.gumroad.com/l/dark-mode-ios-app-icon-pack)
- iOS 26 added a "Clear" icon mode (Liquid Glass) next to Default/Dark/Tinted, and TechCrunch published a customization how-to. Some users complained that "clear" icons are not truly clear. — [TechCrunch, Sept 24, 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/24/how-to-customize-your-iphone-home-screen-for-ios-26s-liquid-glass/); [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/ios-make-app-icons-clear-iphone-home-screen/); [Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/256137570)
- A viral TikTok Liquid Glass "experiment" (pulling Notification Center over a photo) was covered by German press. It is a novelty clip, not an app promotion. — [netzwelt.de](https://www.netzwelt.de/news/246815-ios-26-sorgt-lacher-diesen-kuriosen-trick-solltet-testen.html)

### Inferences
- Since 2022 the "new iOS feature" spike has shrunk and got more paid (Search Ads). A small indie should not count on an OS-launch wave, but should still ship a themed pack plus a tutorial video in the week of each September iOS release.
- iOS 18 "ugly tinted icons" complaints and iOS 26 "Clear isn't really clear" complaints give a contrast-hook opening ("iOS 26 made your phone look like this... here's 2003 instead"). Frutiger Aero's glossy, glassy aesthetic is close to Liquid Glass, which makes a natural "Apple finally caught up to 2006" angle (my inference).
- Locket's sequence is the most replicable for an indie: founder account with an original sound → UGC copies → paid micro-ambassadors → in-house multi-post team.

### Gaps
- I found no Sensor Tower/Appfigures report of an app chart surge tied to iOS 18 tinted icons or iOS 26 Clear icons, and no quantified 2024–2026 TikTok trend data for them.
- I found no revenue figures for Brass, Themify or Color Widgets beyond the 2020 Sensor Tower numbers.
- I found no public data on "Launcher"/Android icon-pack app TikTok campaigns.

## Q3. How consumer/indie apps run TikTok now: slideshows, multi-account "farms", UGC/ambassador payouts, automation

### Takeaway
The dominant 2024–2026 indie playbook is **faceless photo-mode slideshows** posted at high volume (2–5 per day) across several niche accounts, often automated with AI agents. Higher-budget apps add paid micro-creator/ambassador programs. Almost all numeric evidence comes from vendor blogs or founder self-reports, so treat it as directional.

### Cited Findings
- **Stronger (fitness app):** 725 TikToks in 295 days, averaging 2.5/day and up to 5/day, credited with growth toward 700K+ users. The thesis is consistency and quantity, multiple posts per day. **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/the-slideshow-strategy-that-generated-700000-users-in-275-days)
- **Astrology app:** the main account posted 385 slideshows in 105 days, and its multi-account slideshow network reached about 50M views/month. **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/tiktok-multi-account-slideshow-strategy-making-50m-views-per-month/)
- **Humans Anonymous:** 135,937,113 views in 260 days across **7 accounts, each dedicated to one niche/vertical**, and reportedly #1 on launch day (Health). **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/136m-views-in-260-days-on-tiktok-to-reach-1-on-the-appstore)
- **Dumbphone (indie minimalist home-screen-adjacent app):** 20K downloads and $9K MRR in 78 days, with 2.4M+ TikTok views. **[vendor/anecdotal]** — [Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/20k-downloads-and-9k-mrr-in-78-days-how-this-indie-app-turned-minimalism-into-millions-of-views)
- **Kyle's Cardstock + Scanemon:** reportedly about $120K/month combined, largely from a TikTok slideshow strategy. **[secondary/anecdotal]** — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/the-120kmonth-tiktok-slideshow-hack)
- **Oliver Henry's "Larry" (OpenClaw AI agent):** the agent generates slideshows and uploads them as TikTok drafts; the founder adds a trending sound and publishes (about 60 sec/day). Reported 500K views in 5 days, later 8M in a week. MRR is reported inconsistently at $588, $714 and $700–1,000 for Snugly (an interior-design app). **[self-reported via secondary, inconsistent]** — [GrowwStacks](https://growwstacks.com/blog/how-ai-agent-generated-700-mrr-500k-views); [Luminary Lane](https://luminarylane-website.pages.dev/blog/from-larry-to-lane); [Postiz](https://postiz.com/blog/ai-agent-tiktok-views-postiz-api)
- An IndieHackers post describes slideshows taking small apps to "1000s in MRR." Another small dev reported a vibecoded iOS app reaching 100+ downloads and 1,000+ likes via automated slideshows, a modest and realistic early outcome. **[self-reported]** — [IndieHackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-had-no-idea-tiktok-slideshows-could-get-apps-to-1000s-in-mrr-05ed74d49e); [IndieHackers: SlideStorm](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/slidestorm)
- **Posting cadence:** Superscale recommends at least 2 posts/day for conversion impact. Stormy describes multiple posts/day across several accounts and creator fleets running niche accounts. **[vendor]** — [Superscale](https://www.superscale.ai/guides/social-slideshows-growth); [Stormy](https://stormy.ai/blog/tiktok-organic-strategy-viral-app-growth)
- **Multi-account tooling and caveats:** SlideStorm posts to up to 100 TikTok accounts. A "TikTok Slides Spam" Notion guide recommends acquiring and warming up 25+ accounts. TikTok policy risk around coordinated or inauthentic accounts was not verified. — [IndieHackers: SlideStorm](https://www.indiehackers.com/product/slidestorm); [Notion guide](https://grave-mat-e9c.notion.site/TikTok-Slides-Spam-c31cc19ba71c4c8bb79895b3e82925bb)
- **Affiliate networks:** one app runs 100 affiliate accounts with 1B+ views. **[vendor]** — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/this-app-has-100-affiliate-accounts-and-more-than-1bn-views-on-tiktok)
- **Hook-series repetition:** @carlo.digital made 92 versions of one hook ("Secret websites that feel illegal to know") for 58.9M total views. **[vendor]** — [Shortimize: FOMO hooks](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/tiktok-fomo-hooks-driving-millions-of-views)
- **Benchmark for per-view payouts:** TikTok's own Creator Rewards Program pays about $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, for videos of 1 minute or longer only. App ambassador programs pay creators directly (weekly, per the Locket/Airbuds playbook above). — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/how-much-does-TikTok-pay/)
- **Spark Ads:** no theme-app-specific Spark Ads data found.

### Inferences
- For Y2K Home, a realistic indie setup is 2–3 niche accounts (for example a Frutiger Aero aesthetic account, a Y2K/2000s nostalgia account, and a "theme your phone" tutorial account), each posting 1–3 slideshows or short videos daily, with the same hook formats iterated dozens of times.
- Expect 100s, not millions, of downloads from the first weeks. The cited big numbers are survivorship-biased vendor showcases.

## Q4. What separates viral home-screen videos from flat ones; real phone footage vs. mockups

### Takeaway
Evidence favors **transformation and tutorial content shown on a real device**: before→after reveals, step-by-step setup that viewers save, themed by fandom or aesthetic, with a trending or original sound. Direct TikTok A/B evidence for phone-in-hand over rendered mockups is thin. The best quantitative signal is app-store-preview testing, which shows real-person/real-device UGC beating plain screen recordings.

### Cited Findings
- The 2020 viral driver was a tutorial walkthrough (Mogus/Widgetsmith) showing how to get the aesthetic, not an ad. — [mjtsai.com](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2020/09/25/widgetsmith/)
- Locket's spread depended on an **original sound** that UGC creators reused, and on real people showing the widget on their own phones. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/11/locket-an-app-for-sharing-photos-to-friends-homescreens-hits-the-top-of-the-app-store)
- Brass's onboarding leads with the desired end state (finished aesthetic screens), the same "reveal first" principle. — [ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/brass-icon-themes-widgets)
- **"Phone-to-Phone" format:** filming an app in use on one phone from a second phone, rather than a screen recording. It is described as consistently effective for apps and reusable across many videos, but no view data is given. **[vendor opinion]** — [Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/phone-to-phone-tiktok-format)
- **Closest quantitative signal:** StoreMaven 2025 data cited by an agency-linked blog shows a UGC-style preview with a real person plus the screen gave +25% install CVR, versus +12% for screen recording with text overlays. This is App Store preview testing, not TikTok. **[secondary]** — [UGC vs professional app demo](https://collab-only-site.pages.dev/blog/posts/ugc-vs-professional-app-demo-video)
- Shaky phone footage makes viewers scroll away, so stabilize hand-held shots. Screen recordings are double-encoded and look softer. **[vendor]** — [Ulanzi](https://www.ulanzi.com/blogs/guides/film-tiktoks-reels-alone); [Alibaba product insights](https://www.alibaba.com/product-insights/is-screen-recording-on-tiktok-lowering-video-quality-or-just-perception.html)
- Slideshows work especially well for app tutorials and feature demos. — [Microlaunch](https://microlaunch.net/h/how-to-get-more-app-downloads-through-tiktok-marketing)

### Inferences
- High-probability formats for Y2K Home (inferred from the evidence above):
  1. **Before→after transformation**: stock iOS 26 screen, then a fast transition, then a glossy Frutiger Aero/Y2K screen on a real phone in hand.
  2. **"Set up my home screen with me" satisfying tutorial**: icon swaps, widget placement, wallpaper; saves come from the tutorial value.
  3. **Nostalgia hook slideshows**: "Your phone if it was 2004," "POV: your iPhone runs Windows XP vibes," with theme variants per slide.
  4. **Contrast hooks off Apple features**: "iOS 26 Liquid Glass vs. actual 2006 Aero glass."
  5. **Original or trending nostalgic sound** (2000s startup chimes, MSN-style alerts) that UGC can reuse, following the Locket mechanic.
- Use a real phone in hand for the reveal shot, and slideshows or screen captures for volume. Rendered mockups have no supporting evidence.

### Gaps
- No controlled TikTok study compares real-device footage with rendered mockups for app content.
- I found no per-video retention, save or share data for home-screen videos specifically.
