# Make the home screen boot up, not sit still

Y2K Home should replace its static home-screen stills with **short (7–15s) videos that turn a recognizable 2000s sensation into motion and sound, then reveal one of the three themes as the payoff**. The strongest evidence points to three formats. The first is a **"boot-up" install reveal** built on original dial-up, startup-chord and notification sounds. The second is the **before→after home-screen transformation on a real phone**, the format behind the 2020 widget boom. The third is the **Frutiger Aero ambient loop**, the native shape of a hashtag that reportedly grew from 28M to 416M+ views. Below is a prioritized slate of 20 concrete videos. **14 can be rendered entirely from the app's existing React components in Remotion. 6 need real phone footage, at least for the reveal shot.** A two-week plan follows that tests about six lead formats at roughly 1.5 posts per day, along with a per-format scorecard built on 3-second retention, completion, and shares and saves per 1,000 views. The binding constraints are serious. A business account (or any post promoting the app) can only use TikTok's **Commercial Music Library**, the most evocative era sounds and images are owned (the Mac chime and Nokia tune are registered sound marks, and Microsoft owns *Bliss*), and on-screen text must stay inside a conservative safe zone. One caveat applies throughout: **almost every finding comes from search-engine summaries rather than full-page reads** (many hosts were blocked during research), there are no 2025–2026 per-video view counts for these exact formats, and much of the app-marketing evidence comes from vendors selling slideshow tools or from founders reporting their own numbers. Treat figures as directional and treat the idea ranking as a set of hypotheses for testing.

## Why static posts underperform: nostalgia needs a spike, and TikTok rewards watching

Nostalgia is a social emotion. It makes people feel more connected and more loved ([Wildschut et al. 2006, via Southampton CRSI](https://southampton.ac.uk/~crsi/Sedikides%20Wildschut%20Arndt%20%20Routledge%202008%20CDir.pdf)), and in six lab experiments that connectedness **made people willing to pay more for products** ([Lasaleta, Sedikides & Vohs 2014](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:oup:jconrs:doi:10.1086/677227)). But nostalgia is a warm, low-arousal feeling. Berger & Milkman's study of ~7,000 NYT articles found that **high-arousal positive emotion drives sharing and low-arousal emotion suppresses it** ([Wharton PDF](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2011/11/Virality.pdf)). A static image of a pretty home screen produces recognition, but nothing turns that recognition into a share. Every idea below therefore pairs a warm trigger (a sound, an object, an interface) with a **high-arousal beat**: a sudden transformation, a sound you "can hear", a joke, or a "wait, my iPhone can do that?" moment.

The platform pushes the same way. TikTok says user interactions, **including time spent watching, outweigh captions, sounds and hashtags** in For You ranking ([TikTok Newsroom, via secondary summaries](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)). Practitioner consensus, which is not official, puts completion and rewatches first and **shares and saves above likes** ([dev.to 2026](https://dev.to/stklen/tiktok-algorithm-2026-why-shares-and-saves-matter-more-than-likes-2gb4)). A still gives the algorithm almost nothing to measure. A 9-second loop that cuts seamlessly back to its first frame can push average percent watched past 100%.

The feed is also more crowded than it was. Metricool's 2.3M-post study found **average views per TikTok video fell 31% year on year in early 2026 while post volume rose 72%**. It also found that **96% of a video's views arrive within 10 days** ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)). Buffer's 11.4M-post analysis found the **median video gets ~500 views regardless of posting frequency**, and that posting more mainly raises the odds of a breakout ([Social Media Today on Buffer](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-post-frequency-improves-per-post-reach-report-buffer/802443/)). Together these favor exactly what the founder is building toward: **cheap, templated variants that buy many draws at a heavy-tailed outcome**.

The evidence on format is contested. Fanpage Karma found carousels earn an **81% higher engagement rate but only 3% more reach** than video ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/)). Metricool found **video drew ~5x the reach** of carousels in 2026 ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/press-release-tiktok-study-2026/)). The two findings reconcile once you separate the metrics: carousels engage the people who see them, and video reaches more people. Y2K Home should lead with video and keep carousels for comment-bait "pick one" posts, which the existing Playwright still pipeline can already produce.

### What the evidence says about which triggers land

Sound is the strongest trigger in the notes. A viral compilation of MSN, Nokia, dial-up and Windows startup sounds drew comments such as "My whole childhood in a few minutes" ([Newsweek](https://mag.newsweek.com/woman-summons-millennials-childhood-sounds-1892740)). Nokia ringtone remixes became a TikTok trend that also introduced older tech to younger users ([Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/tiktok-time-machine-a-look-at-2000s-nostalgia-trending-on-tiktok/)). "I can hear this image" is an established caption idiom ([Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/rt-if-you-can-hear-this-image)).

On the visual side, Frutiger Aero is the best-documented living aesthetic. Reported views on its hashtag climbed from **28M ([Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tiktok-msn-messenger-windows-vista-noughties)) to ~270M ([Guardian via Magzter](https://magzter.com/stories/Newspaper/The-Guardian/Frutiger-Aero-The-Mid-2000s-Design-Trend-Taking-Social-Media-By-Storm)) to 416M+ ([t3n](https://t3n.de/news/apple-frutiger-aero-gen-z-design-2000er-liquid-glass-1601885/))**. Creators are documented re-skinning their home screens with skeuomorphic icons ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/frutiger-aero-aesthetic-resurgance)). Creative Bloq itself cautions that it is unclear whether this is a "definitive resurgence".

Three other signals matter. The **digicam revival** (#digitalcamera at 124M views in 2022, per [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2022/10/26/gen-z-is-bringing-back-digital-cameras-of-the-early-2000s); Canon PowerShot sales up nearly sevenfold from 2022 to 2025, per [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/18/nx-s1-5896117/retro-digital-camera-analog-reboot)) maps directly onto the Vibe Card widget. **Apple's iOS 26 Liquid Glass**, which PC Gamer said "looks an awful lot like Windows Vista circa 2007" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/windows/apples-new-ui-for-macs-and-iphones-combines-the-optical-qualities-of-glass-with-a-fluidity-only-apple-can-achieve-but-it-sure-looks-like-an-awful-lot-like-windows-vista-circa-2007)), gives the app a timely contrast hook. Finally, the audience includes many people nostalgic for eras they never lived through: a 2026 report puts that share of Gen Z at **65%** ([Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2617505/gen-z-is-embracing-nostalgia-it-never-lived-through-and-millennials-are-to-thank-new-report-says)). So every video has to work as *vibe* for younger viewers and as *memory* for millennials.

Some trends are cooling. One outlet says **McBling hype "petered out by the end of 2024"** ([Daily Dot](https://dailydot.com/best-2024-tiktok-aesthetics)), which is a weak signal that Pink Chrome needs sharper hooks than Aero. Year-specific waves such as January 2026's "2016" moment (TikTok reported a **452% rise in "2016" searches**) peak and fade within weeks ([B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/a-throwback-to-2016-other-tiktok-trends-in-january/)).

"Remotion" below means the founder's React widget and theme components, already rendered to 1080×1920 stills via Playwright, are dropped into a Remotion composition and animated with props. This is an inference worth verifying early: if the Playwright stills come from a web build (react-native-web), the same components should import into Remotion with little change. "Real footage" means a phone filmed in hand, ideally filmed with a second phone (the "phone-to-phone" shot) rather than captured by screen recording.

### Tier A: carry the first two weeks

**1. "I can hear this home screen" (boot-up install).** *Hook text:* "turn your sound on if you remember this 🔊". *Beats (10s):* 0–1s: black screen, green segmented loading bar, original modem screech starts on frame 0. 1–4s: "Installing Y2K Home…" as the bar fills in chunks, with a CRT scanline overlay. 4–5s: an original warm startup chord swells and a white flash fires. 5–8s: the Pink Chrome home screen resolves, the chrome spheres catch a light sweep, jelly icons bounce in one by one. 8–10s: the chrome clock ticks to the current time and the bar resets, looping back to frame 0. *Trigger:* dial-up handshake plus "that startup sound energy", recreated originally. *Why:* era UI sounds produce the strongest recognition response in the notes ([Newsweek](https://mag.newsweek.com/woman-summons-millennials-childhood-sounds-1892740)). The "I can hear this image" idiom shows viewers already frame images as sounds ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/rt-if-you-can-hear-this-image)). Locket's spread ran through an **original sound that other users reused**, including one UK video that hit **5M views in a day** ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/11/locket-an-app-for-sharing-photos-to-friends-homescreens-hits-the-top-of-the-app-store)). An owned boot sound can become the account's signature and a reusable sound for other creators. *Remotion:* fully templatable (`InstallReveal` with props `theme`, `hookText`, `bootSfx`, `loop`). Swap the theme prop for Aero (the bar fills with water) and Aero Night (aurora). Bake in only the original SFX.

**2. Stock iPhone → 2006 transformation.** *Hook text:* "my iPhone in 2026 → my iPhone in 2006". *Beats (12s):* 0–2s: hand holding the phone on a stock home screen, with the hook text. 2–3s: thumb taps the theme, or a whip transition. 3–7s: the Frutiger Aero sky, glossy aqua icons and bubbles fill the screen on the real device. 7–10s: slow push-in on the weather and world-clocks widgets. 10–12s: the phone tilts toward the camera and "Y2K Home" appears as small text. *Trigger:* skeuomorphic glass against flat design (fan sources date the end of Aero to iOS 7 flat design, per [Aesthetics Wiki](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero)). *Why:* the 2020 widget wave was driven by a **tutorial that ended in a reveal**. A walkthrough by Katarina Mogus reportedly passed **25M views**, which is secondary and unverified ([mjtsai](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2020/09/25/widgetsmith/); [KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/ios-14-homescreen-customizations)). App-store preview testing cited by an agency blog found real-person/real-device UGC lifted install conversion **+25% versus +12%** for screen recordings, which is secondary evidence and not measured on TikTok ([source](https://collab-only-site.pages.dev/blog/posts/ugc-vs-professional-app-demo-video)). *Remotion:* hybrid. Film the reveal on a real phone. Remotion can supply the text overlays, safe-zone layout and end card around the clip (`OffthreadVideo` in a template). Stabilize the hand-held footage, because vendors note that shaky shots get scrolled past ([Ulanzi](https://www.ulanzi.com/blogs/guides/film-tiktoks-reels-alone)).

**3. "Apple brought glass back."** *Hook text:* "iOS 26 calls it Liquid Glass. We called it 2007." *Beats (12s):* 0–3s: a real capture of the founder's own stock iOS 26 home screen with "Clear" icons, captioned "'clear' icons that aren't clear". 3–4s: a glass-shatter or water-ripple transition. 4–9s: the Frutiger Aero theme with glossy pill buttons, bubbles drifting and lens flares. 9–12s: caption "glass, but make it the original". *Trigger:* Vista-era glass. *Why:* TechRadar called Liquid Glass Apple's most skeuomorphic update since iOS 6 and likely Aero-influenced ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/phones/iphone/apples-new-liquid-glass-design-puts-the-spotlight-on-skeuomorphism-for-the-first-time-since-ios-6-and-im-all-for-it)). PC Gamer compared it to Vista. Users complained that "Clear" icons are not truly clear ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/how-to/ios-make-app-icons-clear-iphone-home-screen/)). This gives the video a contrast hook tied to a live conversation. *Remotion:* hybrid. The stock-iOS half must be a real capture used only as context. Do not recreate Apple UI as artwork. The Aero half is fully templatable. Keep Apple's name in the caption descriptive rather than implying any affiliation.

**4. "A future that never came" ambient loop.** *Hook text:* "the future we were promised in 2007". *Beats (15s, seamless loop):* 0–3s: slow parallax over original grassy hills, sky and sea. 3–7s: bubbles rise and the camera drifts down to the phone, which floats in the scene with the Frutiger Aero theme. 7–11s: the weather widget's sun glints and a school of fish passes behind the glass. 11–15s: the camera drifts back up and ends on the opening frame. *Trigger:* the Frutiger Aero vocabulary (sky, water, bubbles, glossy glass) and the "anemoia" of a future that never arrived. *Why:* it is the native format of the aesthetic. Slideshows of FA imagery "set to soothing electronic music" are what took the hashtag viral ([Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tiktok-msn-messenger-windows-vista-noughties)). Long ambient FA videos also find audiences on YouTube ([inkl/Guardian](https://inkl.com/news/frutiger-aero-the-windows-screen-saver-design-trend-taking-tiktok-by-storm)). *Remotion:* fully templatable (`AmbientLoop`). Pick a calm ambient track from the CML in-app, or use a licensed or original "Aero-style" bed. Do not use Wii Shop or Windows welcome music, which fan sources call the movement's "anthems" ([shapes.inc](https://shapes.inc/fandom/frutiger-aero)) but which are corporate-owned. Export a carousel version with `remotion still`.

**5. "Burning your summer mix" (Mixtape widget).** *Hook text:* "track 1 of your first burned CD. go." *Beats (10s):* 0–2s: an original CD-R spins into frame with a Sharpie label reading "SUMMER MIX '04". 2–5s: a "burning… 74 min" progress bar fills with an original whirr SFX. 5–8s: the disc slots into the Mixtape widget on the Pink Chrome home screen and the cover art flips in. 8–10s: the scrubber wheel clicks and the text "what's yours? ↓" appears. *Trigger:* burned CDs and mixtapes, which are generic objects with low IP risk. *Why:* 2000s music on TikTok works as an "ideal mix between familiarity and newness" ([Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/tiktok-time-machine-a-look-at-2000s-nostalgia-trending-on-tiktok/)). The memory prompt fits nostalgia's social function ([Wildschut, via Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/yearning-for-yesterday/)). The best comment replies feed reply-to-comment follow-ups (#10 shows the mechanic). *Remotion:* fully templatable (`MixtapeBurn` with `labelText`, `coverArt`, `songTitle`). Song titles shown on screen should be original or fictional. Never imply a real track is playing unless it comes from the CML.

**6. Widget ASMR tour.** *Hook text:* "the sounds your phone should make". *Beats (12s):* 0–2s: a tight crop on the chrome analog clock with a crisp original tick. 2–4s: the Mixtape scrubber wheel turns with click-wheel ticks (a generic wheel, not an iPod silhouette). 4–6s: the Vibe Card takes a shutter snap, a hard white flash and a date stamp. 6–8s: the calendar date is typed by keypad multi-tap. 8–10s: the weather widget's bubble pops. 10–12s: the camera pulls out to the full Pink Chrome screen and the loop restarts. *Trigger:* the *rhythm* of era interactions (click wheel, T9, digicam). *Why:* the trigger inventory in the notes rates original but audibly in-genre UI sounds as the safest high-value asset class ([Newsweek](https://mag.newsweek.com/woman-summons-millennials-childhood-sounds-1892740) for recognition; IP rationale in the constraints section). Tight crops also produce a rewatch-friendly loop. *Remotion:* fully templatable (`WidgetASMR`, one sequence per widget, with SFX keyed to frames). A second variant filmed with real fingers on the phone (phone-to-phone) tests the real-device premium.

**7. "Which one were you?" theme triptych.** *Hook text:* "pick your 2000s phone: 1, 2 or 3". *Beats (9s):* 0–1s: the three phones side by side, tiny. 1–3s: Pink Chrome fills the frame with the label "1: mall at 3pm". 3–5s: Frutiger Aero fills the frame with "2: the screensaver in the computer lab". 5–7s: Aero Night fills the frame with "3: messenger at 2am" (never name MSN). 7–9s: back to the triptych with "comment your number". *Trigger:* identity sorting, the "this is so me" impulse behind tagging. *Why:* "which layout fits your vibe" posts are a live genre on Lemon8, which is anecdotal and has no view data ([Lemon8](https://www.lemon8-app.com/@sincerelyteetech/7583062278367494687?region=us)). Low-effort numeric comments are the cheapest form of engagement, and carousels engage the people who see them ([Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/)). *Remotion:* fully templatable. Render the video and a three-slide still carousel from the same props, and post both to compare formats.

### Tier B: strong, but costlier or less proven

**8. "Your iPhone photos but make it 2006" (Vibe Card digicam).** *Hook text:* "your camera roll if it was 2006". *Beats (10s):* 0–2s: a crisp modern iPhone photo. 2–3s: a shutter sound and hard flash. 3–6s: the same photo appears in the Vibe Card with grain, flash falloff and an orange "'05 11 11" date stamp, and the caption types in. 6–10s: three more photos snap through and the camera pulls back to the home screen. *Trigger:* point-and-shoot digicam. *Why:* #digitalcamera reached 124M views, with "fake the 2000s look on iPhone" tutorials ([PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2022/10/26/gen-z-is-bringing-back-digital-cameras-of-the-early-2000s)). The appeal is "perfection fatigue" ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/lifestyle/the-return-of-digital-cameras-why-gen-z-want-blurry-pictures-again-1.500606241)), and the trend has reached real-world sales ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/18/nx-s1-5896117/retro-digital-camera-analog-reboot)). *Remotion:* hybrid. Use real photos the founder owns, or photos with consent. The flash, grain and date-stamp FX are template props.

**9. "Set up my home screen with me."** *Hook text:* "Y2K home screen in 15 seconds (save this)". *Beats (15s, plus a 40–60s long cut):* 0–1s: the finished screen flashed first, the end state shown up front. 1–4s: open the app and pick Frutiger Aero. 4–8s: apply the icon pack, with fast cuts on taps. 8–11s: long-press, add the Mixtape and clock widgets, place them. 11–13s: set the wallpaper. 13–15s: the full reveal, which returns to the opening frame. *Trigger:* the tutorial-reveal format of the iOS 14 wave. *Why:* saves are high-value signals ([dev.to](https://dev.to/stklen/tiktok-algorithm-2026-why-shares-and-saves-matter-more-than-likes-2gb4)). Tutorials were the unit that went viral in 2020, and Brass's onboarding also leads with the end state ([ScreensDesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/brass-icon-themes-widgets)). Buffer's 1.1M-video study found longer videos tended to get *more* views ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/)), so test a long cut. *Remotion:* not templatable as content, because it needs real on-device footage of iOS's widget-adding flow. Remotion can still frame it with step labels.

**10. "Comment your away message."** *Hook text:* "your away message in 2006 was ___". *Beats (10s):* 0–2s: a generic, original buddy-list window with fake screen names (no butterfly or running-man icons). 2–5s: away messages cycle: "brb mom needs the phone line", "~*~ lyrics u wouldn't get ~*~". 5–7s: an original two-note "pop" and a window shake. 7–10s: the messages land as the Vibe Card caption on the Aero Night home screen with "drop yours ↓". *Trigger:* messenger status culture and its notification "nudge". *Why:* comment prompts asking for a personal memory match how nostalgia works socially ([Wildschut](https://southampton.ac.uk/~crsi/Sedikides%20Wildschut%20Arndt%20%20Routledge%202008%20CDir.pdf)). Replies seed a data-driven series in which top comments are rendered into Vibe Cards. The notes found **no data quantifying whether "comment your ___" prompts raise comment counts**, so this is a hypothesis to test. *Remotion:* fully templatable (`CommentReply` takes an array of comment strings). IP risk is high if the video drifts toward MSN or AIM branding, so keep it generic.

**11. "2am in 2009" (Aero Night).** *Hook text:* "your desktop at 2am in 2009". *Beats (12s):* aurora ripples over a star field; dark glass tiles with neon glyphs fade up one by one; world clocks show three cities at night; a soft glow pulses in sync with the ambient bed; slow push-in, then loop. *Trigger:* Dark Aero, a documented darker sub-genre ([Gurlworld](https://gurlworld.com/frutiger-aero-the-iconic-2000s-tech-and-corporation-aesthetic-and-its-sub-genres/)). *Why:* it reaches an existing community through the "Dark Aero" term (inference), and the late-night mood borrows from the liminal and dreamcore genre ([Yahoo](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/dreamcore-aesthetic-tiktok-unsettling-nostalgia-135245091.html)). "Aero Night" itself has no search history. *Remotion:* fully templatable (`AmbientLoop` with the Aero Night theme).

**12. POV: home from school in 2007.** *Hook text:* "POV: it's 2007, you just got home from school". *Beats (12s):* illustrated original scenes: backpack drop, the "computer room" glow, an original boot chord, then a cut to the phone in hand showing the Frutiger Aero theme and the caption "this is the phone you deserved". *Trigger:* everyday-scene POV. *Why:* POV recreations are a prominent nostalgia format ([Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/redes-sociales-y-comunidades/ultima-tendencia-tiktok-nostalgia-anos-2000-millennials-recuerdan-gen-z-idealiza-modas-entonces)). The best-documented example is a class-of-2004 video with 29M+ views, reported in 2021 and therefore dated ([Spartan Shield](https://spartanshield.org/49890/feature/digital-nostalgia-the-rare-aesthetics-trend-transports-tiktok-users-back-to-their-childhood/)). *Remotion:* templatable if the scenes are illustrated or rendered. A real-phone ending likely converts better (#2 rationale).

**13. "If you know this sound…"** *Hook text:* "if you know this sound, your home screen should look like this". *Beats (9s):* 0–3s: an unbranded, illustrated flip phone buzzes with an **original** polyphonic ringtone. 3–4s: the flip opens. 4–9s: a match-cut from the flip screen to the Pink Chrome home screen with jelly icons. *Trigger:* ringtones and the "I'm this old" age-signalling idiom ([KYM](https://knowyourmeme.com/editorials/collections/20-nostalgic-images-from-your-childhood-to-make-you-feel-old)). *Why:* Nokia ringtone remixes trended as a shared millennial touchstone ([Hypebot](https://www.hypebot.com/tiktok-time-machine-a-look-at-2000s-nostalgia-trending-on-tiktok/)). The Nokia tune itself is a registered sound mark ([USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/soundmarks/trademark-sound-mark-examples)), so the melody must be new while the chiptune *timbre* stays. *Remotion:* fully templatable.

**14. Founder: "I built the phone I wanted in 2006."** *Hook text:* "I missed glossy buttons so much I built an app". *Beats (15s):* face to camera for 2s, then the phone in hand cycling through all three themes, with a final line about it being an indie app. *Trigger:* maker authenticity plus all three aesthetics. *Why:* Locket's founder-run account was the spark before UGC copies, at about 100K views in a couple of days ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/11/locket-an-app-for-sharing-photos-to-friends-homescreens-hits-the-top-of-the-app-store)). It also adds a trust layer that faceless templates lack. *Remotion:* needs real footage. Post it once, early, and pin it.

### Tier C: cheap variants and reactive slots

**15. Ironic dolphin.** *Hook/caption:* "me after 9h 41m of screen time" over an original glossy dolphin-and-bubbles Aero scene that pans to the home screen. *Beats (7s):* scene, caption, then a slow zoom into the screen-time widget area and loop. *Why:* juxtaposing an FA dolphin with a dark caption is a documented 2024 format ([W Collective](https://wcollectivemagazine.cargo.site/frutiger-aero-editorial)). Humor supplies the arousal spike. *Remotion:* fully, with captions as a prop, so batch-render 10 captions.

**16. "Your Top 8 apps."** *Hook:* "Top 8 apps. who made the cut?" *Beats (9s):* a Pink Chrome glitter frame with eight app slots that fill one by one with jelly icons (generic categories), ending on "rank yours ↓". *Why:* the profile "Top 8" concept is free to use even though the MySpace name and logo are marks (trigger table in the notes); it is a ranking-comment prompt. *Remotion:* fully, plus a carousel.

**17. Texting in 2005.** *Hook:* "typing 'brb' in 2005 took 7 presses". *Beats (8s):* keypad multi-tap SFX while T9 text types into the Vibe Card caption, then the Pink Chrome reveal. *Why:* T9 and keypad interactions rate high on recognition and low on IP risk (trigger inventory). *Remotion:* fully.

**18. Screensaver mode.** *Hook:* "your phone after 5 minutes idle in 2005". *Beats (12s):* original 3D tubes or floating bubbles take over the screen, a tap dissolves them into the Aero home screen, and the screen loops back to idle. *Why:* screensavers are a high-recognition trigger. The specific Microsoft visuals carry IP risk, so these must be original geometry. *Remotion:* fully, as a natural seamless loop.

**19. "Redesigned in 2007."** *Hook:* "what if the Notes / Camera / Music icons were made in 2007". *Beats (10s):* a flat generic glyph morphs into a glossy aqua icon, three times, then the full icon pack appears on the home screen. *Why:* creator @uxbyant's "reimagine modern app icons in Frutiger Aero" format is documented, though no view counts are available ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/frutiger-aero-aesthetic-resurgance)). Etsy and Gumroad icon-pack sales show buyers pay for curated packs ([Redmondpie](https://redmondpie.com/?p=438583)). *Remotion:* fully. **Use generic categories only**, never real brand app icons.

**20. Year-wave reactive slot.** *Hook:* "[trending year] is back, so is your home screen". *Beats (10s):* a year counter rolls back, era-appropriate original FX play (scanlines, flash, glossy bars), the world-clocks widget reads "it's 3:07pm in 2004", and the matching theme is revealed. *Why:* year-nostalgia waves can spike sharply (+452% "2016" searches) and fade within weeks ([B&T](https://www.bandt.com.au/a-throwback-to-2016-other-tiktok-trends-in-january/); [The Week](https://theweek.com/culture-life/nostalgia-2016-social-media-trend)). A template keyed by a `year` prop lets the account react within hours. *Remotion:* fully. Also reserve this slot for each September iOS release, when theme apps historically spike ([Appfigures](https://appfigures.com/resources/insights/20220916/1-ios-16-propells-widgets-to-the-top-of-the-app-store)).

### Six compositions cover fourteen ideas

The Remotion-native ideas collapse into about **six reusable compositions**:

- `InstallReveal` (#1, #13, #17, #20)
- `AmbientLoop` (#4, #11, #18)
- `WidgetASMR` (#6)
- `MixtapeBurn` and `CommentReply` (#5, #10, #16)
- `Triptych` (#7)
- `CaptionMeme` (#15, #19)

Each should take a Zod-typed props object, roughly `{theme, hookText, keyword, sfx, ctaText, loop, variantId}`. Remotion supports input props, default props and data-driven duration through `calculateMetadata()` ([Remotion props resolution](https://remotion.dev/docs/props-resolution)). A `variants.json` file fed through `selectComposition` and `renderMedia()` batch-renders the hook variants one at a time locally, as the docs recommend ([Remotion dataset render](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/dataset-render)). Writing `variantId` into the filename lets the founder join renders to TikTok analytics later. `remotion still` produces the carousel slides from the same compositions.

Remotion's **Free License covers individuals and companies with up to 3 employees, including automation**. Larger companies pay $0.01/render with a $100/month minimum, according to secondary summaries that should be verified on the official page ([Remotion License](https://remotion.dev/license)). Lambda is optional, and the official cost example puts a warm render at roughly **$0.001** ([Remotion cost example](https://convert.remotion.dev/docs/lambda/cost-example)). Keep the Remotion code outside `src/app/`, since every file there is an Expo Router route.

## A two-week plan that tests six formats, not twenty

Two weeks is too short to judge twenty formats fairly. Metricool says a post's views are essentially complete by day 10, and medians need several posts per format. The plan therefore concentrates volume on the Tier A formats, gives each about **3–4 posts with different hook text**, and keeps Tier B and C as single probes. The cadence is about **1–2 videos per day plus 2–3 carousels per week on one account**, roughly 24 posts in total. Vendors recommend at least two posts per day ([Superscale](https://www.superscale.ai/guides/social-slideshows-growth), vendor), and case studies such as Stronger's 2.5 posts per day are vendor-reported ([Shortimize](https://shortimize.com/blog/the-slideshow-strategy-that-generated-700000-users-in-275-days)). Metricool's finding that daily posting "does not guarantee better distribution" argues against raw volume for its own sake.

Hold off on multi-account "farms" until the winning hooks are known. Third-party guides warn that duplicate clips across accounts lose reach and that automation-like behavior can trigger flags. Those thresholds, such as "~3 accounts per device", are folklore with no TikTok source ([Shortimize](https://www.shortimize.com/blog/how-to-manage-multiple-tiktok-accounts)).

At day 14 each Tier A format has 2–4 data points. Days 15–24 then shift the volume toward the top two formats by median, with about 10 hook variants each on the same body. That follows the "hook → reveal → CTA" loop practitioners describe ([Stormy](https://stormy.ai/blog/tiktok-app-marketing-ugc-faceless-viral-hooks), vendor). Put the theme keyword ("frutiger aero home screen", "y2k iphone theme") in the on-screen text, the caption and any voiceover. Practitioners report that TikTok search indexes OCR text and transcripts as well as captions, though TikTok has not documented it ([Metricool SEO](https://metricool.com/tiktok-seo/)).

## The scorecard: judge formats on medians, shares and saves

TikTok's native analytics expose **average watch time, "watched full video" percentage, total play time and the retention curve** ([Darkroom](https://www.darkroomagency.com/observatory/tiktok-algorithm-guide-2026-everything-we-know-about-how-videos-are-ranked)). Every other benchmark in circulation is vendor folklore. Published 3-second "hook rate" targets range from "50%+ functional" to "80–90% strong", none with a methodology ([TTSVibes](https://insights.ttsvibes.com/?p=533); [ShortsFast](https://shortsfast.com/blog/faceless-shorts-hooks-retention-2026/)). Judge formats against the account's own baseline, using **medians across at least 3–5 posts**, read 7–10 days after posting.

| Metric | Where it comes from | What it tells you | Working rule (own-baseline) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3s retention | Retention curve at 3s | Hook text + frame 0 strength | Rank hooks within a format; drop any hook below the account median |
| Avg % watched | Avg watch time ÷ length | Body holds attention; loops work | >100% means the loop is earning rewatches; keep loop formats that hit it |
| Watched full video % | Native analytics | Completion, the top practitioner signal | Compare formats of similar length only |
| Shares / 1K views | Shares ÷ views × 1000 | Nostalgia converting to "send to a friend" | Primary format-level KPI |
| Saves / 1K views | Saves ÷ views × 1000 | Tutorial and reference value | Primary for #9, #19 |
| Comments / 1K views | Comments ÷ views × 1000 | Memory-prompt effectiveness | Primary for #5, #7, #10, #16 |
| Profile visits / 1K views | Native analytics | Interest in the app behind the video | Leading proxy for installs |
| App Store installs (by day) | App Store Connect, dated to posts | Actual business outcome | Views ≠ downloads ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-run-a-faceless-ai-tools-tiktok-just-shipped-the-playbook-with-the-actual-2026-monetization-stack-3-sourced-case-studies-6fe30c2f50)) |

The decision rule: keep a format when its median **shares + saves per 1,000 views** beats the account median and its 3-second retention is at or above median. Kill or re-hook a format that misses both after 3 posts. Expect hundreds of downloads, not millions, in the first weeks. The big published case studies are survivorship-biased vendor showcases, and one small developer's honest outcome was "100+ downloads" ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/i-had-no-idea-tiktok-slideshows-could-get-apps-to-1000s-in-mrr-05ed74d49e)).

## Constraints that shape every render: music, marks and margins

**Music.** Business accounts can only pick from the **Commercial Music Library**, even for organic, unpaid posts. A personal account posting promotional content falls under the same rule ([Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/why-can-i-only-use-commercial-sounds-on-tiktok); [Third Chair](https://usethirdchair.com/blog/tiktok-commercial-music-library-what-it-covers-and-what-it-doesn-t)). Trending 2000s hits are therefore effectively off-limits. Reported CML terms (about a 60-second cap, TikTok-only use, no alterations) and the April 2025 policy date come from secondary sources and need confirming against TikTok's own text ([Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/tiktok)). Independently licensed tracks need a licence that covers commercial TikTok use plus TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation ([Dynamoi](https://dynamoi.com/learn/faqs/can-i-use-my-own-music-on-tiktok-business-account)). Brands have been sued over this: Sony v. DSW is cited over brand posts that used Sony recordings ([Foxi Music](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/commercial-music-licensing-tiktok-guide/)).

The practical pattern is to **bake only original SFX (and original or properly licensed music) into Remotion renders, then add a CML bed in-app if needed**. Check whether layering your own SFX under a CML track counts as an "alteration". Building a house library of original sounds (modem, boot chord, nudge-pop, T9, click wheel, CD burn, shutter) is the single highest-leverage audio investment, because those sounds are both the trigger and fully owned.

**Sounds and images that are owned.** The **Mac startup chime is a registered US trademark** (Reg. No. 4,257,783, per [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2012-12-12-apples-mac-startup-chime-now-a-registered-trademark.html)). The **Nokia tune is a USPTO sound-mark example** ([USPTO](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/soundmarks/trademark-sound-mark-examples)). Windows startup recordings are copyrighted, and the Windows 95 sound was composed by Brian Eno ([Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/features/audio-logos?ssrid=ssr)). **Microsoft owns *Bliss*** ([Wikipedia](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Bliss_(image))). **Nintendo has blocked about 1,300 YouTube uploads of its music** ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/nintendo-has-blocked-over-1300-youtube-videos-featuring-its-soundtracks)), which rules out Wii-style menu music. Commonplace or functional sounds cannot be registered as marks ([TMCA](https://thetmca.com/whats-that-sound-it-might-just-be-a-trademark/)), so a *genre* of beep, chime or modem screech is open to anyone while a specific famous one is not.

The same principle applies to visuals. Descriptive language such as "Aero-era", "Vista vibes", "flip-phone energy" and "that startup sound you're hearing in your head" is low risk. Logos, OS screenshots presented as the product, the Start orb, MSN or AIM icons, iPod silhouettes, Tamagotchi shapes, doll likenesses, and product names like "Windows XP theme" are not. The trademark status of the click wheel, Tamagotchi, the MSN and AIM sounds, and the Sidekick was **not verified** and should be checked on USPTO or EUIPO. A pixel-close recreation of OS chrome can read as trade-dress copying even if every asset is redrawn. None of this is legal advice.

**Safe zones.** TikTok publishes no official pixel spec, and third-party estimates conflict: bottom margins range from 250 to 480px ([trymypost](https://www.trymypost.com/blog/social-media-safe-zones-templates-2026); [Quso](https://quso.ai/blog/tiktok-dimensions)). Use the conservative rectangle as a template constant: **top 200px, bottom 480px, left 60px, right 160px**, which places hook text and widgets within roughly x 60–920 and y 200–1440. Add a debug overlay prop so every variant is checked before export. Put the hook in frame 0 and never open on a logo or title card.

**Evidence quality.** Every note behind this report reports blocked page fetches (Dazed, Creative Bloq, TechCrunch, Sensor Tower, TikTok Newsroom, remotion.dev and others), so the numbers come from **search-result summaries rather than full pages**. Three groups of figures should be treated as the weakest:

- **Vendor or anecdotal** figures, mostly from vendors selling slideshow and TikTok tools: Shortimize case studies (Stronger, Humans Anonymous, Rayz AI, Locket's "250M views"), Stormy, Superscale, all hook-rate thresholds, StoreMaven's +25% via an agency blog, and the "phone-to-phone" recommendation.
- **Self-reported** figures from founders and developers: Widgetsmith's downloads and the "Larry" agent's MRR.
- **Unverified secondary** figures: Mogus's 25M views, the Vevo, Talker and DesignRush survey percentages, and the CML terms.

No source measured nostalgia's effect on TikTok shares directly, compared real-phone footage with rendered mockups for app content, or gave 2025–2026 per-video views for Aero home-screen videos. The tier ranking is an evidence-weighted bet, not a measured result.

## Conclusion

Y2K Home's advantage is not that its themes look nostalgic. Static images already show that. The advantage is that the founder can make the themes **sound** and **move** like 2006, owns every asset involved, and can generate dozens of variants from components that already exist. The research points to an asymmetry worth exploiting. The most powerful nostalgia cues (startup chimes, ringtones, messenger pings) are exactly the ones big brands cannot use and creators get claimed for. An indie app that commissions a recognizable *original* boot sound and makes it the account's audio signature turns an IP constraint into a moat, and gives other creators a sound to reuse, which is the Locket mechanic.

The second implication is about timing. Liquid Glass has made "glass UI" a mainstream conversation again, and each September iOS release is a recurring demand spike for theme apps. A templated pipeline that can ship a "new iOS, same 2006" video on launch day is worth more than any single viral hit. Build the six compositions, run the two-week test honestly against medians, and let shares and saves per 1,000 views, not view counts, decide which nostalgia gets scaled.
